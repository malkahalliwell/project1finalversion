Why I love Milan

This summer I got to spend 2 months living and working in Milan. It is a fabulous city with so much to see and do. Here are some highlights for when you visit!

What to do in Milan

Fashion

If you love fashion, Milan is the place to be! It is one of the fashion capitals of the world and has many high-end shopping districts. You can find everything from designer boutiques to vintage shops.

The Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II

The Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II is a beautiful shopping mall in Milan. It is one of the oldest shopping malls in the world and is home to many luxury brands. The architecture is stunning, with a glass ceiling and intricate mosaics on the floor.

The Galleria includes global high-end fashion brands, Italian designers and boutiques

Art

Milan has something for every kind of art lover, from old masterpieces to modern art. You can spend a whole day going from one museum to the next.

Classical Art

Milan is home to some of the most famous art in the world. You can go see The Last Supper, painted by Leonardo da Vinci. It is on a wall in the Santa Maria delle Grazie church and tickets sell out fast, so book them well before your trip!

Modern Art

If you prefer modern art, Milan has plenty of galleries and museums with new exhibits all the time. To see what is on right now, check out this list of current exhibitions in Milan.

Food

Milan has amazing food, and you can eat well at every meal. Pasta, pizza, pastries and gelato are everywhere, and there are some local dishes you have to try.

Classic Milanese Dishes

  • Risotto alla Milanese: a creamy rice dish made with saffron
  • Cotoletta alla Milanese: a breaded and fried veal cutlet
  • Ossobuco: slow-cooked veal shank, often served with risotto
  • Panettone: a sweet bread that started in Milan, especially popular at Christmas

Gluten Free in Milan

I am celiac, and Milan was easy to eat in! There are tons of gluten free options, including pasta, pizza and desserts. Most restaurants know what celiac means and take it seriously, but it is still a good idea to tell your server about it when you order.

Pastries and Gelato

Stop by a pasticceria (pastry shop) for something sweet, and end the day with gelato. You will find a gelateria on almost every street.
