Travel Destination: Yellowstone National Park

Yellowstone National Park is a great destination, home to lots of natural beauty and wildlife, and is great for those who enjoy the outdoors.

It is one of my favorite places I have traveled to in the United States. I visited in August/September of 2021 and experienced a lot. This page is dedicated to why I love Yellowstone, and what makes it such a great destination.

Why I Love Yellowstone:

Yellowstone is a large national park that offers lots of unique geological features and diverse wildlife, with a comfortable balance of activities for all types of visitors. I love the fact that Yellowstone is a place where you can see wildlife in their natural habitat, go on great hikes, and see some interesting natural oddities that you can't find anywhere else. It definitely feels like a getaway, because there is so much open space and so many things to do, that you can really feel like you are much more isolated than going on a trip to a busy city.

Top Sights to See:

Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone

The Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone is a must-see when visiting Yellowstone National Park. It is a large canyon that is over 20 miles long and up to 1,200 feet deep. The canyon is home to several waterfalls, including the famous Lower Falls, which is one of the most photographed waterfalls in the park. There are several viewpoints along the canyon that offer great views.

Old Faithful

Old Faithful is a geyser in Yellowstone National Park that erupts approximately every 90 minutes. It is one of the most popular attractions in the park and offers a unique opportunity to witness the power of nature, as the geyser shoots 150+ feet into the air.

Grand Prismatic Spring

The Grand Prismatic Spring is the largest hot spring in the United States and one of the most iconic features of Yellowstone National Park. It is known for its vibrant colors, which are caused by different types of bacteria that live in the water. The spring is approximately 120 feet deep and 300 feet wide.
