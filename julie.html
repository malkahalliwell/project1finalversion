Norway

About

Norway is a place filled with beautiful fjords, mountains, and vibrant blue water. It has a unique cultural connection to vikings, trolls and the maritime industry. Many people visit Norway in an attempt to see the Northern Lights. Beyond this astral phenomenon, Norway has plenty of unique architecture and art. Popular cities to visit in Norway include Oslo, Bergen, Trondheim, Stavanger, Kristiansand, and the Lofoten islands.
